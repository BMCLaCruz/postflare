## When to use
Use when something verifiable happened: a round download number (10k, 100k, 1M), a top-10 rank in a category, an App Store "App of the Day" / "Game of the Day" / editorial story, a Google Play "Editors' Choice" or feature, a rating milestone, or an anniversary. Milestones spread because people like to share an underdog's win, especially when the post makes them part of it. Post within a day or two while it's still true (chart ranks change hourly). Skip vanity milestones nobody else cares about (e.g. 500 commits).

## Structure
1. **Verify the claim.** Download numbers from App Store Connect / Google Play Console, not estimates. Chart ranks need the country, category and chart: "#4 in Health & Fitness (Free), US App Store", with a dated screenshot. "Featured" only if Apple or Google actually featured it, and say where (Today tab story, App of the Day, a collection).
2. **Line 1: the milestone plainly, framed around people**, ≤ 70 characters, no emoji at the start. "100,000 of you now water your plants with Pocket Plants."
3. **The journey in 2–3 beats:** where it started (first build, first 10 users, the rejection), a hard moment, where it is now. One sentence each.
4. **The thanks, specific:** beta testers, the person who made the first fan art, the subreddit that found it, a single review that kept you going.
5. **The visual:** the proof screenshot (store feature card, chart, console graph with numbers you're comfortable sharing) or a then-vs-now pair (first prototype vs today).
6. **What's next in one line**, optional, and a light invitation ("if you've been around since the 1.0 days, say hi"). No hard CTA; newcomers find the app through the store link in a follow-up.

## Platform notes
- **X:** 280 chars. Milestone + journey in the main post with the proof image; the thanks and "what's next" as thread follow-ups. Store link in a follow-up, never the main post (reach drops and the X API bills URL posts much more).
- **Bluesky:** 300 graphemes; same split. The indie community there boosts genuine wins.
- **Threads:** 500 chars fits milestone + journey + thanks; one topic tag ("Indie Apps").
- **Mastodon:** 500 chars, CamelCase hashtags (#IndieDev). Humble tone matters most here.
- **Instagram:** carousel journey (first sketch, first build, launch day, today, thank-you frame), up to 10 frames (Postflare supports 10+). Caption fold at ~125 chars. Alt text.
- **TikTok / Reels / Shorts:** 9:16 "then vs now" with on-screen text in the first 1–2 seconds ("Two years ago this app had 3 users"). 15–30 seconds.
- **LinkedIn:** 3,000 chars, fold after ~210 chars. Lessons learned format fits; first comment holds the link via a follow-up. Post on the personal profile; reshare from the page.
- **Reddit:** only where milestone posts are allowed (often r/SideProject, r/indiegames, your own sub); lead with what you learned and real numbers, not the brag.
- **Apple trademarks:** use "App Store" and "App of the Day" as plain text. Don't use the Apple logo, don't alter Apple's badges, and don't imply endorsement beyond what happened ("featured by Apple on the App Store" is accurate; "Apple's favorite app" isn't). Check Apple's App Store marketing guidelines before using any badge or "featured" artwork, and use only official assets, unmodified. Same for Google Play's Editors' Choice badge.

## Hooks
- **Credit the crowd:** "{Number} of you now {do the thing} with {app name}." — the milestone becomes the audience's achievement.
- **Then and now:** "{Time} ago: {small start}. Today: {milestone}." — the contrast tells the story without bragging.
- **The honest one:** "{Milestone}. I almost {gave up/shipped the wrong thing} at {point}." — vulnerability makes it shareable, if it's true.
- **The feature:** "{App name} is {exact feature placement} today." — plain statement; let the screenshot do the excitement.

## Avoid
- Unverifiable or inflated numbers ("#1 app!" without country, category or chart).
- Calling a store search result or "You Might Also Like" placement a "feature".
- Revenue flexes or comparisons with competitors.
- Apple logo, altered badges or wording that suggests Apple or Google endorsement.
- Fake humility ("so humbled to announce…") followed by a pure pitch.
- Hard CTAs and fake urgency ("download before we hit 200k").
- Engagement bait ("like to help us hit 1M").
- Waiting a week: chart ranks and features are dated claims.

## Checklist
- [ ] The milestone number comes from App Store Connect, Play Console or a dated store screenshot.
- [ ] Chart claims name the country, category and chart (free/paid/grossing).
- [ ] "Featured" is used only for real editorial placement, described accurately.
- [ ] Line 1 frames the milestone around the users.
- [ ] The journey has 2–3 concrete beats, and the thanks names specific people or groups (with their OK).
- [ ] Store badges or feature artwork are official and unmodified; no Apple logo.
- [ ] Store link is in a follow-up on X, LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube, with a campaign token.
- [ ] Wording is adapted per platform and within limits.
